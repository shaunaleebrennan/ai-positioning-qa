Evidence-led messaging review

Make positioning feedback discussable.

An AI-assisted positioning review for B2B messaging. Score what the asset actually communicates, trace every observation to evidence, and keep the final judgement with a human.

01 / Principle

A diagnostic, not market validation.

The score helps prioritise questions and revisions. It does not prove message-market fit; only customer and performance evidence can do that.

Local
Your source copy stays local by default.

This app sends no data anywhere. You decide whether to copy a generated prompt into an external AI tool. Remove confidential, personal, or unapproved material and check that tool’s privacy terms first.

02

Add the exact source

Paste the copy unchanged. Exact quotations make the review auditable and help expose unsupported inferences.

One asset per review Plain text only No confidential data

03

Score the eight dimensions

Use 1–5 anchors, cite the source, record what is missing, then make one focused recommendation. Responsible AI may be marked N/A when no AI claim is present.

1 Missing or misleading 3 Adequate but incomplete 5 Clear and well supported

The review dimensions are preparing…

Each dimension requires a score or not-applicable selection, supporting evidence, missing context, confidence, and a recommendation.
